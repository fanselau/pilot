<objective>
Remove retry completely from Pilot. Jobs are disposable — they either succeed or fail. No retries, no resetToPending, no second chances.

Purpose: Simplify the job lifecycle. The step-continuation model (Phase 73) handles gaps/hung/failed within a single job run. Once a job terminates, it's done. Users queue new jobs instead of retrying old ones.

Output: Clean codebase with no retry concept — only succeed, fail, unblock, or queue-new.
</objective>
